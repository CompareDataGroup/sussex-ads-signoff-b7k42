10%Of the subscription, every month, for each builder they bring. Ten builders at £49 is £49 a month.
£500A month on top for accounts over 100k.
2 postsA month, by contract, for the over 100k accounts.
  1. We approach them and they pass the same verification as any builder.
  2. They get their own link and code, so every sign-up is counted.
